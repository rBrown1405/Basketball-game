# assets/audio/test

The test pack: tones made by tools/audio/testpack.js, used by the audio console's test buttons.

Every file in this folder needs a row below before tools/audio/pack.js will pack it: the file, where it came
from (a link), who made it, and its license. Only licenses the game can ship are accepted: CC0 or public domain,
CC BY (credit the author in the row), or made by this project. CC BY-NC, BY-ND and BY-SA, "personal use",
"royalty free but no redistribution" (the Sonniss GDC bundles, the BBC archive) and anything unclear are out.
